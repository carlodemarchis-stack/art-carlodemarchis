tempospazio

Siamo frammenti di tempo sospesi nello spazio.

A fragile dance between the visible and the fleeting.

Time doesn't flow — it bends, slips, disappears. Space is the stage where we dissolve, a flickering echo of our passing. Bodies in motion, minds adrift, lives slipping past the present moment.

These images are not posed. They hold waiting, disorientation, the rush. A way to witness the invisible, the unspoken, the nearly forgotten.

We remain suspended, inside a time that refuses to be held.
