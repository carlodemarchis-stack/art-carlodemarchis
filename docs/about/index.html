photography beyond photography

As a photographer's son, I have been raised and educated with black & white film-based photography.

With a background in tech and photography I am now creating impact projects using AI-generated photography: black and white realistic photos that I use for storytelling and social provocation work.

I am also extremely fascinated by photography as that moment in time in a fluid sequence of images — a frame, but also a physical space.

The use of AI-generated photography is, in this case, just a creative tool, like any other.
